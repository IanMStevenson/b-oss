Privacy Policy — b-mobile for Blipfoto

b-mobile for Blipfoto is an Android app for browsing, posting to and getting notifications from your Blipfoto journal. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and how to have it removed.

Last updated: October 2026

Who is responsible

b-mobile for Blipfoto is published by Salient Point and developed by Ian Stevenson as part of b-oss. It is an independent app built on Blipfoto's public API. Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.

What stays on your device

  • Your Blipfoto access tokens, held in Android's secure storage (the Keystore). They are used to talk to Blipfoto and are never shown or logged.
  • Your app settings, the list of members you have hidden, drafts, and photos queued for upload.
  • A temporary cache of pictures you have looked at, which expires after 15 minutes and which Android can clear at any time.

None of this is sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app removes it.

What is sent to Blipfoto

The app is a client for Blipfoto, so everything you do in it goes to Blipfoto's servers under Blipfoto's own privacy policy: signing in, browsing, posting and editing entries, uploading photos, comments, stars, favourites, follows and reports. If you add a location to an entry, or use the Nearby tab, the coordinates are sent to Blipfoto as part of that request. The developer does not receive a copy.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo for an entry.
  • Location (approximate or precise) — only when you tap my-location on a map or open Nearby. Your position is used for that request and is not stored by the app.
  • Notifications — optional; to alert you to new comments and activity, and to remind you to post.
  • Internet — to reach Blipfoto and the services below.

The app does not ask for access to your photo library; you pick photos with Android's own photo picker, which shares only the ones you choose.

Maps

Map screens load map tiles from MapTiler. When you open a map, MapTiler receives your device's IP address and the map area you are viewing, under MapTiler's privacy policy. Nothing is sent to MapTiler until you open a map.

Notifications (optional)

If you turn notifications on, the app registers with b-push, a small service run by the developer on Cloudflare Workers. To check for new activity it stores:

  • your Blipfoto user ID;
  • a read-only Blipfoto access token, encrypted at rest (it can read, but not post, change or delete anything);
  • your device's push token and platform, which Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging issues so a message can reach your phone;
  • which kinds of notification you chose, how often to check, and the last counts of unread comments and notifications.

b-push only reads counts of unread items from Blipfoto, never their content, and never writes to Blipfoto. The push messages it sends are delivered via Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging and contain no comment or notification text, only which kind of activity occurred and your Blipfoto username. Server logs contain no tokens or request bodies. As hosts, Cloudflare and Google process technical data such as IP addresses under their own policies.

Deleting it: turning notifications off for an account, or disconnecting the account in the app, deletes its b-push record immediately. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the record is deleted automatically the next time b-push tries to reach your device and Google reports it gone. To have a record removed sooner, contact me and quote your Blipfoto username.

What the app does not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no crash-reporting service of its own. Google Play may report crash and performance data (Android vitals) to the developer for devices where you have allowed that, under Google's policies. The developer does not sell or share your data. Apart from the optional notification service above, the app has no server of its own.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Use of Blipfoto is subject to Blipfoto's own terms and age requirements.

Your rights

Everything the app holds is on your device and under your control, apart from the optional b-push record described above, which you can delete yourself by turning notifications off or by asking me. Data held by Blipfoto is managed through Blipfoto.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date at the top.
