Which backup tool is right for you?

b-oss offers two ways to back up your blipfoto journal. Here's how to choose.

Quick guide

Choose b-ark-chrome if you're on Mac or Linux, you usually post from your computer, or you want the simplest possible setup — one click from the Chrome Web Store, no installer, no security warnings, and silent automatic updates.

Choose b-ark if you regularly post from your phone or tablet and want your backup to happen on a timer, automatically, regardless of whether you open a browser. As long as your Windows PC is running, b-ark will keep your archive up to date.

b-ark-chrome

Chrome extension. Windows, Mac and Linux. Best if you post from your computer.

b-ark

Windows desktop app. Best if you post from your phone or tablet.

b-ark-chrome : Backup & Archive

Back up your blips via the Chrome extension — Windows, Mac and Linux.

b-ark-chrome is a Chrome extension that backs up your blipfoto journal automatically. It captures new blips the moment you publish them and checks for any gaps each time you visit blipfoto — no installer, no timer to configure.

  • Backup on publish. Captures new blips within seconds of posting from your computer.
  • Cross-platform. Works on Windows, Mac and Linux wherever Chrome or Edge runs.
  • Simple setup. One click from the Chrome Web Store — no installer, no security warnings.
  • Silent updates. Chrome keeps it up to date automatically.

b-ark : Backup & Archive

Archive your blips on your own device (Windows).

b-ark makes a local copy of every blip you've ever posted (images, text and metadata) on your own machine, automatically keeping it up to date. Browse offline, publish to the web, or hand over to AI tools to explore and create new formats.

  • Backup. A local copy of every blip, updated automatically.
  • View. Browse your archive offline in the built-in viewer.
  • Publish. Copy the backup folder to any web host to publish.
  • AI-ready. README.md for AI tools: Use AI to build photobooks, slideshows, statistics, custom sites.
