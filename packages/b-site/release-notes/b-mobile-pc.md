## 1.5.1 - 2026-11-10

New: a photo picked from your files now sets the entry's date from when it was taken. Fixed: Fit to screen and Compact now work in a wide window.

## 1.5.0 - 2026-11-08

New: an entry's extra photos now show under the photo and in full screen. The full-screen photo can no longer be clipped, and its keyboard zoom steps are gentler. When b-mobile starts with Windows it now opens hidden in the tray instead of showing its window.

## 1.4.0 - 2026-11-06

First release: b-mobile for Windows, from the Microsoft Store. Browse, post, and read your notifications and comments on your PC. It can run from the tray and tell you about new notifications, remind you to post, start when you sign in, and take a photo with your webcam.
