## 1.0.9 - 2026-11-08

Zooming in the photo viewer is smoother and more predictable: sensible zoom steps, drag only when zoomed in, and no jump at fit size.
Backups now pause a fixed 100 ms between fetches.

## 1.0.6 - 2026-10-23

• A notice now explains why a journal needs attention, with Sign in, Retry and Dismiss buttons where they apply.
• Entries that failed to download are retried on later backups, and gaps are filled in.
• A finished backup with entries outstanding no longer restarts every time you visit Blipfoto.
• Thumbnails show properly while a backup is running.

## 1.0.5 - 2026-09-08

New: back up your Blipfoto journal straight from Chrome, with no separate app. It also backs up Original and Extra images, with a viewer for browsing Extra images, and backups are faster, newest posts first.
