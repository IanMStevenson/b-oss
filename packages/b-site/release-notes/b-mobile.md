## 1.4.0 - 2026-11-08

New: an entry's extra photos now show under the photo and in full screen. You can change an entry's date when editing, there is a Compact photo size, and a keyboard works throughout. Also now on Windows, from the Microsoft Store.

## 1.3.0 - 2026-11-05

New: a bottom bar to step through the grid you opened an entry from, and tap a profile picture to see it full size. Notifications and comments now refresh when you return to the app, and the publish confirmation thumbnail is centred.

## 1.2.2 - 2026-11-03

Fixed: starred and favourited entries now show as given, the publish confirmation shows your entry's thumbnail, the status bar is green on Android 10 and older, and app links and shared photos work on older devices.

## 1.2.1 - 2026-11-02

Fixed: tapping a tag now lists its entries. When something can't load, you now get a clear message (offline, or Blipfoto not answering) with Try again and a link to the status page.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-02

New: satellite view on the Map and when picking a location.

Changed:
• Publishing is now immediate and ends with a confirmation screen. There is no upload queue or Uploads screen any more.
• The menu is reordered, with icons, your account at the top and the version at the bottom.
• Settings is regrouped: Appearance, Reminders, Details & journal.
• Settings and Help always show a back arrow.

Fixed: default thumbnail crop, and clearer errors when publishing fails.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-31

New: Dark and Mid themes. A photo picked from your gallery offers its stored location. You can preview and choose the thumbnail, including when replacing a photo. The map has a new look.
Fixed: your chosen thumbnail was being ignored by Blipfoto, and signing in a new account could leave you on the sign-in screen.

## 1.0.2 - 2026-10-26

• When a list can't load, it says whether you seem to be offline or Blipfoto can't be reached, and you can swipe down or tap Try again.
• The map and location picker say when the map can't be shown, instead of a blank area.
• Fixed: Help showed the wrong version number.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-23

• Swiping an entry now works like a photo gallery: left for the next (newer) entry, right for the previous one.
• Edit entry shows the entry's thumbnail with a Replace image button.
• Fixed: editing an older entry no longer seems to save a new title or description that Blipfoto then ignores. You are only offered what can be changed.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-20

First release. Browse Blipfoto (Recent, Following, Popular, Milestones, New Blippers, Nearby), search, and see entries on a map. Post from your camera or gallery. Star, favourite, comment, reply and follow. Use several accounts, hide members, and report entries, comments and members. Optional notifications and a daily reminder to post.
